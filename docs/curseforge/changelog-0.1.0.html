Kinora Replay 0.1.0: first release

For Minecraft 26.2 with NeoForge 26.2.0.88 or newer. Needs Java 25 (the version Minecraft 26.2 comes with). Client only.

Making videos needs FFmpeg, a free extra program. See the setup steps on the mod page. Recording, watching, editing, photos and image sequences work without it.

Record

  • Press F6 to record while you play, in singleplayer or on any server (the server does not need Kinora).
  • Markers are added by themselves for deaths, kills, explosions, advancements and more; F7 adds your own.
  • Replay buffer: F8 saves the last few minutes, even if you were not recording.
  • Recordings survive a game crash.

Watch

  • Open recordings from the Replays button on the title screen.
  • Pause, rewind, slow motion, frame by frame; jump between markers.
  • Free camera, follow any player or mob, or orbit around them.
  • Photo mode: 4K, 8K or 16K stills.
  • Player names can be hidden (V).

Film

  • Camera editor (E): place camera points and the camera glides between them.
  • Ready-made moves: orbit, dolly zoom, crane, fly-through, whip pan, reveal, push-in, turntable.
  • Look-at, follow cams, camera shake, speed ramps, freeze frames, reverse.
  • Color grading, vignette, film grain, bloom, letterbox, depth of field with autofocus.
  • Change the time of day and weather in a shot.
  • Live cut: switch between up to nine cameras while the replay plays.
  • Titles, auto-director, undo, project versions, and sharing a replay with a friend in one file.

Render

  • Perfectly timed frames: smooth video no matter how fast your PC is.
  • MP4 (H.264, H.265, AV1), fast GPU encoding on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel, ProRes, WebM, PNG and OpenEXR sequences.
  • Up to 120 fps, motion blur, supersampling, and frames bigger than your screen.
  • Game sounds as the camera hears them, plus optional music.
  • 360° and 3D video, ready for YouTube.
  • Render queue: stop with Esc and resume later.

Works with

  • Sodium and Iris shader packs.
  • Distant Horizons: far terrain shows in replays.
  • OpenGL and Vulkan.

For mod developers

  • An API (LGPL-3.0) lets other mods add their own data to recordings and react to replays. See the GitHub page.
